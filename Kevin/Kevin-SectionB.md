## Problem Statement
To use a pair of scissors, it is required for you to have a thumb, to hold the scissors and separate the blades; and digit one (index finger) for support and added strength. Because of this, people who are missing their thumb and index finger on their dominant hand have difficulty holding and operating a pair of scissors, causing discomfort, and difficulty having a good hold on the scissors. No existing pair of scissors accommodate for these missing fingers, as most, if not all, pairs of scissors require the thumb. There is a need to design a pair of scissors, or redesign an existing pair, so people who are missing those digits can reliably and safely articulate the blades with little to no difficulty, and to be able to cut a variety of materials with the scissors, while keeping the cost of the scissors low, the overall size of the scissors the same as a normal pair, making the scissor grips ergonomic and comfterble to hold, and making sure the scissors are durable and sharp.
