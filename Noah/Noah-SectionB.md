## Problem Statement  
People who are missing their thumb and index finger are unable to comfortably and practically use scissors, as they have finger holes requiring a thumb. Opening and closing the blades without a thumb is uncomfortable, impractical, and unstable to hold. No scissors on the market (both standard scissors and various accessible scissors) are suitable for someone missing a thumb and index finger. There is a need to design a practical, comfortable, stable, and ergonomic pair of scissors for people who are lacking their thumb and index finger.
