# Adaptive Product Design Project
Accessible product design project for Engineering Capstone

# Assignment

## The Challenge
Everyday products often rely on a user's ability to grip, pinch, and apply force with their thumb and index finger. How could an existing product be redesigned for someone who is missing these digits on one hand?

Working in pairs, design and develop an adaptation to one existing product that helps your assigned persona complete a specific task. Your group will choose a product, investigate the user's needs, generate three feasible concepts, and develop your selected design into a testable prototype.

## The user
Your starting persona is missing the thumb and index finger on one hand and retains the middle, ring, and little fingers.
Develop this persona by identifying:
- Which hand is affected, their goals, and the context in which they use the product.
- Relevant abilities, preferences, and difficulties.
- Information you know, assumptions you are making, and the questions requiring investigation.  

Do not assume that people with the same missing digits have the same strength, movement, sensation, or needs.
Clearly label a fictional persona and any unverified details.

| **Product**  | **Task your redesign should support**                                                                |
|----------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Scissors | Open and close the blades and guide blunt-tip scissors along straight and curves lines on paper |

## Design requirements
Your solution must:
- Adapt an existing product while preserving its essential function.
- Address the needs of your specific persona and task.
- Consider hand contact, control, attachment, and deliberate release.
- Use materials and manufacturing processes available within the class.
- Develop through testing and at least two documented revisions.
- Allow comparison with the original product using the same test conditions.
